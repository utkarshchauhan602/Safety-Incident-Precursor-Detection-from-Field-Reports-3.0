IMPORTANT DATA LIMITATION
The 50,000 examples are synthetic language variations for phrasing coverage,
local response retrieval and QA. They are not real incident reports and do not
establish real-world model accuracy. Operational validation still requires
representative, labelled site reports and linked incident outcomes.

SAFETY LIMITATION
This remains a decision-support prototype, not a certified safety system.
For immediate danger, activate the site's emergency plan and emergency services.
Do not wait for a chatbot response. Competent HSE personnel must verify reports,
controls, risk levels and any precursor alerts.
