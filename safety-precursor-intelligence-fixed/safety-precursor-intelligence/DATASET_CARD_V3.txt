SAFETY PRECURSOR INTELLIGENCE 3.0 — CONVERSATION DATASET CARD
=============================================================

File: spi/data/humanised_hse_conversations.jsonl
Rows: 50,000 (one JSON object per line)
Hazard groups: 10 groups x 5,000 examples each
Format: JSON Lines / UTF-8
Purpose: conversational phrasing coverage, local acknowledgement retrieval,
         regression testing and demonstration of humanised worker language.

The corpus covers electrical contact, unconsciousness, fire, gas release,
chemical spills, working at height, machine guarding, slips/trips, PPE and
vehicle/pedestrian interactions. Each row contains a synthetic worker-style
observation, a non-judgmental acknowledgement, and one clarifying question.

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
---------------------
* All 50,000 examples are generated synthetic language variations. They are
  NOT 50,000 real safety reports, incidents, workers, sites or observations.
* This is not evidence of model accuracy, real-world generalisation, or a
  validated training set. It is not a replacement for labelled site data.
* Safety instructions are not learned from these examples. The chatbot's
  emergency triage and immediate response guidance remain rule-first and are
  sourced from the curated playbooks in spi/knowledge.py.
* The examples do not represent every language, dialect, industry or hazard.
  Review and extend them with domain experts before operational use.
* Do not use this dataset to infer worker blame, intent, medical conditions,
  legal compliance or site risk without competent human review.

Regenerate deterministically with:
    python scripts/build_humanised_corpus.py

Run tests with:
    python -m pytest -q
